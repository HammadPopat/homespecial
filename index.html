Our Heart & Philosophy

Why Murtuza Started Cooking For The City

We got tired of ordering from restaurants that dumped liters of processed red chili oil and MSG into dishes just to mask stale meat and instant gravy bases.

In our kitchen, every morning starts at 6:30 AM with fresh produce from the local mandi. Onions are patiently caramelized till golden honey, tomatoes are stewed without artificial purée, and garlic is crushed right as the pot warms up.

Never Frozen, Never Reheated

Curries are made fresh in the morning for lunch, and separate batches at 5 PM for dinner.

Real Himalayan Pink Salt & Desi Ghee
